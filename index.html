Selling your home? You'll need an electrical COC.

A property can't transfer without a valid electrical Certificate of Compliance, issued by a registered electrician. Most transfers need one issued within the last two years. We match you with an electrician who does COC inspections and the repairs needed to pass.

Questions people ask

Does it cost anything to use Vaal Electricians?

No. Getting a quote through us is free. You only pay the electrician for the job, and only if you accept their quote.

Are you the electrician?

No. Vaal Electricians is a referral service. We pass your job to independent electricians who work in your area. The electrician you choose quotes you directly and does the work.

Can the electrician issue a COC?

Yes. We work with electricians registered to issue Certificates of Compliance. Tell us it's for a COC when you fill in the form.

The whole street has no power. Should I call?

Usually not. If your neighbours are also off, it's most likely a municipal or Eskom outage, which an electrician can't fix. Check with Emfuleni, Midvaal or Eskom first. If only your property is off, we can help.

How fast will someone call me?

We aim to have an electrician call you back within 30 minutes during working hours. For emergencies, calling us is the fastest route.

Who will see my details?

Only the electricians we match you with for this job, up to 2. We never sell your details to marketers. Read our privacy policy.
